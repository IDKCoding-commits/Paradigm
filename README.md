Paradigm is a game heavily inspired by ULTRAKILL and the many roguelikes I've played.

As you explore the brightly lit halls of the Lab, your progress will be hampered by enemies of all kinds.
Your only hope of survival are your trusty guns and the powerful Artifacts you've found along the way. 
You fight your way through hordes of enemies and end your rampage only when the final boss is crushed.

Yet, as you complete another run, and come face to face with the final boss again, they are not the same. 
Your enemy wields all of the artifacts and weapons you used against it, and mimics the fighting style of your previous run. 

The final boss of this game is not the same, repetitive fight. Instead, every time you complete a run, you leave behind your previous character, as a new Iteration takes on the Lab. 
It is this previous character that you fight at the end of your rampage, and every time you win, it gets harder and harder to do it again.
